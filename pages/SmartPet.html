SmartPet

SmartPet is an aspect of SmartHub that assists you with pet care. SmartPet provides an automatic pet doors, litter cleaning, feeding, and pet cleaning.

Pet Door

The automation aspect of SmartHub allows for an automatic pet door installed in your front and back door. You pet will have a special collar that hooks up to the door. When they get near the door, it recognizes the collar and opens automatically.

Litter Cleaning

When your pets litter needs to be cleaned, automation occurs. Your pets litter box is connected to a pipe that reaches below your house. Automation empties the litter and replaces it through an automatic fill up device.

Pet Feeding

With SmartPet, your pets food bowl is automatically refilled with the smart bowl senses the food is low.
